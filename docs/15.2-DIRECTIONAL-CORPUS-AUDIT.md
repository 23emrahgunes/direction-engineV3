# V3.15.2 Directional Corpus Audit

Audit date: 2026-09-16

## Summary

No validated historical Directional training corpus is currently committed to the repository.
Runtime databases and reports are intentionally excluded from Git, and the accepted V3.8 state
explicitly recorded all twelve model buckets as unpromoted. Therefore V3.15.2 must not fabricate
models, coefficients, calibration, labels, or profitability evidence.

The V3.15/V3.15.1 Directional shadow samples are not valid predictive-performance evidence because
the runtime did not yet have complete official PTB, feature, model, calibration, executable pricing,
and settlement lineage.

## Lineage and leakage assessment

- No row is usable for training until it has immutable decision-time PTB, external features,
  model/config hashes, executable benchmark, and later verified official outcome.
- Proxy Binance spot/depth observations are not official references or settlement labels.
- Polymarket books are permitted only for executable pricing and benchmarks, not default
  Directional alpha features.
- All future records must be persisted before outcome is known and later receive a separate
  official outcome attachment.
